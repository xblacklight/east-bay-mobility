Alameda & Contra Costa

Meet you at the door. Get you home after.

East Bay Mobility is a small Oakland dispatch for wheelchair, walker, and companion rides to dialysis, clinics, and hospital discharges. We quote the trip before the van leaves, and we do not pretend to cover the whole Bay at 7 in the morning.

What the desk will do

  • Come through the first door and walk at the rider’s pace.
  • Secure the chair the rider already sits in, when it is safe to ride that way.
  • Read the price back before anyone is loaded.
  • Call when a clinic runs long, before the return changes.

What we will not do

  • Emergency transport, lights, or a nurse in the vehicle.
  • Oxygen management or monitoring of an unstable rider.
  • A curb drop at a campus when nobody named the entrance.
  • A fare that appears for the first time at the end of the driveway.

Five rides. One standard.

The vehicle changes. The habit does not: door, securement, a named return, and a quote you heard already.

Wheelchair & scooter

Lift van, four-point tie-down, lap belt. The chair stays the seat when that is the safe way to ride.

What the unit should send

Stairs

A flight with a rail can be a scheduled two-person assist. A flight with no rail and no weight-bearing is a different job.

When we say no

Not a fit

If the rider must stay flat, or needs clinical care en route, say so on the first call. Some days we have a gurney van. Some days we do not.

01 — The day

How a trip actually moves.

  1. You name the rider and the door.

    Mobility, stairs, campus, and who will meet the van. A hospital name alone is not a pickup.

  2. Dispatch quotes, then confirms.

    Miles, vehicle, wait time, and a caregiver seat are on the quote. You hear the number before we roll.

  3. The driver meets the person.

    Not the curb, unless you asked for the curb. Securement is checked before the wheels move.

  4. The return is already a plan.

    If the physician is late, the driver calls the desk. The family hears about a change before it is a surprise charge.

Campuses are large. We ask which one.

These are places East Bay families already confuse on the phone. Naming the campus is part of booking, not a detail for the driver to guess.

Alta Bates Summit

The Ashby campus in Berkeley and the Summit campus in Oakland are not the same door.

Kaiser Permanente

Oakland, San Leandro, Richmond, Fremont, and Walnut Creek are different trips. “Kaiser” is not a pickup.

John Muir

Walnut Creek Medical Center and Concord Medical Center sit on different lead times for this desk.

UCSF

Benioff Children’s in Oakland is not UCSF at Parnassus or Mission Bay. Cross-bay is booked ahead.

Alameda Health System

Highland in Oakland and San Leandro Hospital are both in the core. Still say which lobby.

Eden & Washington

Eden in Castro Valley is core. Washington in Fremont is real, and it is a day-ahead trip.

Desk hours

5:30a–8:00p, seven days.

Dialysis mornings start earlier by standing order. After 8 we keep a line for discharges already on the book, not for a new same-night trip across the county.
