Coverage

Close enough to be on time.

A desk that promises the entire Bay before dawn will miss someone. Ours is based in Oakland. Same-day rides are strongest along the bay spine. Farther east, and across the bridge, we book the day before so the van is not a rumor.

Schematic, not a navigation chart. Copper is the desk. Deep green is the same-day core. Muted green is day-ahead.

Same-day still depends on the board. A full morning is a full morning, even in Oakland. Call by late morning for an afternoon ride in the core. Early discharges and dialysis belong on yesterday’s book.
