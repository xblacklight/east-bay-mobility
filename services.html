The street the agency already owns.

East Bay Mobility works on operations, safety, and transportation technology for public agencies. The product is a decision a traffic engineer can defend: a timing change, a concept, a system the city can run, or a written opinion.

01

Signals and operations

Phasing, timing, coordination, detection, and the controller and cabinet that have to carry the plan. Includes support for new or modified signals, corridor retiming, and the field conditions that a model will miss: a detector that failed, a side street that grew, a railroad or fire station the coordination never accounted for.

Transit signal priority and emergency-vehicle preemption belong here when the agency intends to operate them. Specifying a system nobody will maintain is not the assignment.

02

Safety and multimodal streets

Collision history, speed, sight lines, crosswalk placement, bicycle movements through a signal, and the seconds a protected turn takes away from someone walking. The work is for public works and traffic staff who need a recommendation they can take to a council, a capital program, or a design consultant.

Pedestrian and bicycle safety are part of the traffic engineering, not a separate brochure.

03

Intelligent transportation systems

Communications, detection, advanced traffic management, connected signals, and the data an operator can use. Rafat Raie’s record includes leading the first cloud-based emergency-vehicle preemption deployment in the United States, and direction of the Contra Costa Transportation Authority’s Digital MOTIONS Master Plan.

Automated-vehicle readiness is treated as an agency problem: signal hardware, communications, curb rules, and what staff will do when the technology arrives. The firm does not sell vehicles.

04

Parking technology

Downtown curb management is an operations problem. Meter type, payment, sensors, enforcement, and the difference between a lot and an on-street space all change what an agency should buy. As Walnut Creek’s city traffic engineer, Rafat Raie took that distinction to the City Council when the city moved to single-space meters.

05

Grants and staff support

Grant applications fail when the scope is vaguer than the street. The firm writes the engineering content of a request — location, deficiency, proposed work, and what the agency will operate afterward — and provides senior traffic engineering for cities that need that judgment on contract.

06

Litigation support

Review of signal timing, sight distance, signing, striping, and collision patterns for counsel. The opinion stays inside the engineering record.
