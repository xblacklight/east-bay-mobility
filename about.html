About the desk

A small board, on purpose.

Too many rides end at the curb of a hospital the driver has never walked into. East Bay Mobility is the opposite habit: learn the entrance, meet the person, leave enough time, and turn work down when saying yes would make the rest of the board late.

The door, not the curb

Door-through-door means we come inside the first door, walk to the vehicle, and go as far as the destination lobby or unit entrance. It does not mean clinical care inside the appointment.

The quote before the wheels

Wait time, stairs, vehicle, and a caregiver seat are part of the price. If the clinic runs long, dispatch calls before that price moves.

East Bay first

Alameda County and west Contra Costa are the core. The rest of Contra Costa, the Tri-Valley, and San Francisco are real trips with an earlier booking, not a slogan.

Questions the desk expects

How soon can you come?

Call by late morning for an afternoon ride inside the core, if a van is free. Dialysis and early discharges need the day before. Far East County, the Tri-Valley, Fremont, and San Francisco are booked ahead. If the board is full, we say full.

Do you bill Medi-Cal or Medicare?

No. This desk is private-pay and facility-account. If a health plan covers non-emergency transportation, call that line first. We will not send an invoice that pretends insurance was billed.

How is this different from East Bay Paratransit?

East Bay Paratransit is the public ADA service established by AC Transit and BART for riders who cannot use the regular bus or train. It has its own certification and booking rules, and it is the right system for many people. East Bay Mobility is a private dispatch for a tighter window, a facility account, or a trip that the public service is not set up to do. We will say so when Paratransit is the better fit.

Can the wheelchair stay under the rider?

Yes, when the chair fits the van and is safe to ride in. We use a four-point tie-down and a lap belt. If a transfer would be safer, that decision is made before the appointment day.

Do you wait during the appointment?

Wait time is written on the quote. A return after a short visit can include a wait. A dialysis shift or a long clinic day is usually a return window, not a driver parked for four hours unless you asked for that and it is on the quote.

What if the physician is running late?

The driver calls dispatch before the return changes. You hear the new time, and any change in price, before it happens.

Can a caregiver ride along?

One caregiver rides when the vehicle has a spare belt. A second person has to be on the quote. Children who need a car seat need that said at booking.

Is this a real transportation company?

No. This is a practice website for a concept brand. See the note below. Do not use it to arrange a real ride.

Colophon

East Bay Mobility is a concept and practice website. It is not a licensed carrier, not a Medi-Cal provider, and not affiliated with East Bay Paratransit, AC Transit, BART, or any hospital named on these pages. The phone number uses the reserved 555 exchange. The email uses the reserved .example domain. Hospitals and cities are real places, described so the fictional desk has to operate the way a careful East Bay desk actually would: two customers (families and facilities), a geography small enough to be on time, and a hard line between a ride and clinical care.

The booking form stores a practice request in this browser only. Nothing is transmitted to a dispatcher.
