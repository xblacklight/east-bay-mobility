The firm

A city traffic engineer’s practice.

East Bay Mobility is the municipal transportation consultancy led by Rafat Raie, T.E. It opened as his own firm in 2026, after a career inside city engineering departments and in front of agency staff who have to keep the signals running.

Rafat Raie, T.E.

Mr. Raie was City Traffic Engineer for the City of Walnut Creek from 1990 to 2018. He came to that job from the City of San Leandro, where he was Associate Traffic Engineer from 1988 to 1990. UC Berkeley’s Institute of Transportation Studies lists him as a Traffic Engineer and as an instructor in the TechTransfer program since 2001, teaching planning, signal design and operations, multimodal systems, bicycle and pedestrian safety, transit operations, parking, and transportation technology.

His technology work includes leading implementation of the first cloud-based emergency-vehicle preemption system deployed in the United States. UC Berkeley also credits him with directing development of the Contra Costa Transportation Authority’s Digital MOTIONS Master Plan, a framework for digital infrastructure and data-driven traffic management.

The firm’s work is municipal transportation consulting: technology integration and deployment, autonomous-driving systems as a question for the agency that owns the street, grant applications, and litigation support.

Who the work is for

Public works directors, city traffic engineers, and transportation managers who need senior help on a signal system, a safety problem, a technology deployment, or a grant. Regional agencies and transit operators belong in the same conversation when the issue crosses a city limit or a signal has to serve a bus. Counsel belongs here when the question is the engineering record.

Names of cities other than Walnut Creek and San Leandro are not listed as clients. A city appears on this site only when the public record ties Mr. Raie to that job.
